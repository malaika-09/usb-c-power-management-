Multi-Layer Board Layout & Routing

📋 PCB Overview

Board Name: USB-C Power Management Hub

Layer Count: 4-layer PCB (recommended for high-speed USB 3.0)

Board Size: Optimized for compact form factor

Design Tool: KiCad 7.x/8.x

🎨 Layer Stack-up

Layer 1 (Top Layer - Signal + Components)

Function: Component placement and primary signal routing

  • USB-C connector and downstream USB Type-A connectors
  • Power management ICs and passives
  • Hub controller IC and support components
  • High-speed USB 3.0 differential pairs
  • Critical signal traces
  • Silkscreen and solder mask

Layer 2 (Inner Layer 1 - Ground Plane)

Function: Continuous ground plane for return paths and shielding

  • Solid ground plane (minimal splits)
  • Provides low-impedance return path for signals
  • EMI shielding between layers
  • Heat spreading for thermal management

Layer 3 (Inner Layer 2 - Power Plane)

Function: Power distribution planes

  • 5V power plane (primary)
  • 3.3V power plane section
  • 1.8V power plane section (if needed)
  • Power plane splits for isolation
  • Low-impedance power distribution

Layer 4 (Bottom Layer - Signal + Ground)

Function: Additional routing and ground pour

  • Secondary signal routing
  • Power traces and distribution
  • Bottom-side components (if any)
  • Ground fill for continuous ground plane
  • Silkscreen and solder mask

📐 Design Specifications

📏 Dimensions

  • Board Thickness: 1.6mm (standard)
  • Copper Weight: 1 oz (35µm) on all layers
  • Form Factor: Compact rectangular

🔧 Trace Specifications

  • Min Trace Width: 6 mil (0.15mm)
  • Min Spacing: 6 mil (0.15mm)
  • Power Traces: 20-40 mil (0.5-1.0mm)

⚡ High-Speed Routing

  • USB 3.0 Pairs: 90Ω differential
  • USB 2.0 Pairs: 90Ω differential
  • Length Matching: ±5 mils within pair

🕳️ Vias

  • Standard Via: 0.3mm drill, 0.6mm pad
  • Power Via: 0.5mm drill, 0.8mm pad
  • Via Stitching: Every 5mm on ground

🔌 Component Placement Strategy

Placement Zones:

  • Input Zone: USB-C connector, PD controller, input protection at board edge
  • Power Zone: Buck converters, LDOs, bulk capacitors in central area
  • Hub Zone: USB hub controller IC with crystal and decoupling
  • Output Zone: USB Type-A connectors at opposite edge with power switches

🎯 Critical Routing Guidelines

USB 3.0 SuperSpeed Routing:

  • ✅ Differential pair routing with 90Ω controlled impedance
  • ✅ Keep pairs together, minimize length difference (<5 mils)
  • ✅ Avoid vias in differential pairs when possible
  • ✅ Reference to solid ground plane on Layer 2
  • ✅ Minimum 3× trace width spacing from other signals
  • ✅ No 90° angles (use 45° or curved)

USB 2.0 Routing:

  • ✅ 90Ω differential pair impedance
  • ✅ Keep D+ and D- traces parallel and matched in length
  • ✅ Series termination resistors (22-33Ω) close to source

Power Routing:

  • ✅ Wide traces for high current paths (≥40 mil for 3A+)
  • ✅ Star ground connection at input
  • ✅ Separate analog and digital grounds (connected at one point)
  • ✅ Power plane pour on Layer 3 with stitching vias

🛡️ EMI/EMC Considerations

  • Ground Stitching: Via stitching every 5mm around high-speed signals
  • Guard Traces: Ground guard traces around USB differential pairs
  • Filtering: Common-mode chokes on USB data lines
  • Decoupling: Multiple capacitors (bulk + ceramic) near each IC
  • Shielding: Ground pour on top and bottom layers where possible
  • Keep-out Zones: No traces under USB connectors (reduce EMI)

🌡️ Thermal Management

  • Heat Sinking: Large copper pours under power ICs
  • Thermal Vias: Multiple thermal vias under IC thermal pads
  • Airflow: Component placement allows natural convection
  • Hot Spots: Power switches and buck converter ICs properly cooled

🔍 Design Rules

Clearances

  • Trace-to-trace: 6 mil
  • Trace-to-pad: 6 mil
  • High voltage: 15 mil

Annular Rings

  • Minimum: 4 mil
  • Preferred: 6 mil
  • Ensures reliability

Solder Mask

  • Clearance: 3 mil
  • Dam width: 8 mil
  • Color: Green (standard)

Silkscreen

  • Line width: 6 mil min
  • Text height: 40 mil min
  • Color: White on green

✅ Manufacturing Specifications

  • PCB Standard: IPC Class 2 or Class 3
  • Surface Finish: ENIG (Electroless Nickel Immersion Gold) or HASL
  • Solder Mask: Green LPI (Liquid Photo-Imageable)
  • Silkscreen: White legend on top and bottom
  • Testing: Electrical test (flying probe or fixture)
  • Impedance Control: ±10% tolerance on 90Ω differential pairs

📦 Assembly Notes

  • SMT assembly for most components (compact, automated)
  • Through-hole USB connectors for mechanical strength
  • Reflow profile: Lead-free (SAC305) at 245°C peak
  • Manual assembly of USB connectors after reflow
  • AOI (Automated Optical Inspection) recommended
  • Functional testing of all USB ports and power rails
