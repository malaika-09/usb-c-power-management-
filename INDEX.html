📋 Project Overview

This documentation package contains a complete technical design for a USB-C Power Management System with integrated USB 3.0 Hub Controller. The design implements a modern, hierarchical approach to power delivery and USB data management.

🔋 USB-C PD Support

USB Power Delivery 2.0/3.0 with voltage negotiation from 5V to 20V, supporting up to 100W power delivery.

⚡ Multi-Rail Power

Efficient voltage regulation providing 5V, 3.3V, and 1.8V rails with high-efficiency buck converters and LDOs.

🚀 USB 3.0 Hub

Full USB 3.0 SuperSpeed support (5 Gbps) with backward compatibility for USB 2.0 and USB 1.1 devices.

🔌 Multi-Port Output

4-7 downstream USB Type-A ports with individual power control and protection, each supporting up to 1.5A.

🛡️ Complete Protection

Overcurrent, overvoltage, ESD, and thermal protection on all power and data lines for maximum reliability.

📏 Compact Design

4-layer PCB optimized for space efficiency with proper thermal management and signal integrity.

📐 Layer Stackup

4-layer PCB (Signal/Ground/Power/Signal+Ground) with controlled impedance traces

Up to 60W total from USB PD input with efficient regulation
